# Adaptive Security

> Adaptive Security is the human security platform for the AI era. We protect
> organizations against AI-powered attacks — deepfakes, voice cloning, phishing,
> and more — through interactive training, realistic simulations, email security,
> and AI governance. Trusted by 1,000+ companies worldwide.

## Products

- [Security Awareness Training](https://adaptivesecurity.com/security-awareness-training): Modern, interactive training for AI, deepfakes, voice scams, and more. 1,000+ modules, fully editable, branded to you.
- [Phishing Simulations](https://adaptivesecurity.com/products/phishing-simulation): Simulate email, voice, SMS, and deepfake attacks org-wide. AI-powered scenarios built on real employee data.
- [Cloud Email Security](https://adaptivesecurity.com/email-security): Multi-layer AI detection built on top of Google and Microsoft — catching the threats they miss.
- [AI Governance](https://adaptivesecurity.com/ai-governance): Real-time visibility, policy enforcement, and in-browser coaching for safe AI tool use.

## Platform

- [Risk Management](https://adaptivesecurity.com/risk): Individual employee risk scores based on training, simulation, and real-world behavior.
- [Reporting](https://adaptivesecurity.com/reporting): Completion rates, threat breakdowns, and audit-ready exports for every compliance framework.
- [Dynamic Groups](https://adaptivesecurity.com/dynamic-groups): Automatically enroll employees in the right training based on role, risk, and behavior.
- [Integrations](https://adaptivesecurity.com/integrations): Connect to your HRIS, SSO, and security stack in minutes.
